2. DXF LAYERS (how to use)
--------------------------
OUTLINE         Cut outer profile 100 x 100
DRILL_M2_CLR    Drill through Ø2.4  (M2 clearance)
CB_M2           Counterbore Ø4.0 x 3.0 deep FROM BOTTOM face
DRILL_M3_CLR    Drill through Ø3.4  (M3 clearance)
CB_M3           Counterbore Ø5.5 x 3.5 deep FROM BOTTOM face
TAPDRILL_1_4    Drill Ø5.10 (#7) through, then TAP 1/4"-20 UNC through
TAP_1_4_20      Location / major reference only (do not cut as final hole)
TAPDRILL_3_8    Drill Ø7.94 (5/16") through, then TAP 3/8"-16 UNC through
TAP_3_8_16      Location / major reference only (do not cut as final hole)
REF_CAMERA      Construction only — DO NOT MACHINE (camera footprint ghost)

IMPORTANT:
- Machine counterbores on the BOTTOM face (stand side).
- Camera sits on the TOP face.
- Camera mounting screws go UP through plate into camera.
- Camera thread depth is only 3.0 mm — do not use long screws.

4. ORIENTATION
--------------
- Camera lens / front of camera points toward +Y (top of plate).
- Camera body footprint approx X=35.5–64.5, Y=35.0–65.0 (REF_CAMERA layer).
- Digitek C-stand 1/4" or 3/8" stud screws into center tapped holes from BOTTOM.

5. HARDWARE (customer supply — for reference)
---------------------------------------------

Camera M3 : M3 x 5 or M3 x 6 socket head (2–3 pcs) — engagement ≤ 2.5 mm
Stand     : Digitek 1/4"-20 or 3/8"-16 stud into tapped holes

7. INSPECTION
-------------
